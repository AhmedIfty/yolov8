You can query any of these sub-classes:

Sharp objects:
  knife, scissors, fork, pin (thumbtack/push pin), needle, razor (razor blade), nail,
  axe (hatchet), screw

Medical:
  mask (face mask/surgical mask), glove (medical glove/gloves),
  test tube (blood tube/vacutainer/lab tube),
  syringe, medicine package (drug package/blister pack/pill strip/medicine pack)

Plastic:
  plastic bottle (PET bottle/water bottle)

Paper:
  paper (tissue/napkin/toilet paper/paper towel)

Glass:
  glass bottle

Metal:
  metal can (aluminum can/soda can/beer can/tin can), electronics (electronic device/circuit board)

Notes:
- For medical queries, CLIP only checks YOLO boxes labeled “medical”.
- For sharp-object queries, CLIP only checks YOLO boxes labeled “sharp-object”.
- For plastic/paper/glass/metal queries, CLIP is gated to those super-classes respectively.
- If a box doesn’t meet the confidence/margin thresholds, it won’t be labeled.
